Terms for attendees

Please review before you register.

General
  1. Attendees must be a current account of Dix Performance and be an invited guest.
  2. Account must be current and in good standing to attend the trade show.
  3. Show orders meeting the minimum commitment must be submitted between February 16th and February 17th, 2024. Orders received after February 19th, 2024 will not be eligible for the exclusive show discounts and incentives.
  4. Once registered, any cancellations must be received before travel is booked or you will be charged the full amount of the travel & accommodations.
  5. The purchase of crate engines, superchargers or transmissions will not count towards the qualifying minimum order amount.
  6. Not meeting the minimum order or payment requirements results in a charge back of the costs associated with attending the show.
  7. Failure to maintain good standing may result in the full outstanding order amount due immediately, plus the cost of the flight, hotel, and any door prizes won.
Attendee obligations
  • Refrain from conduct that would damage the image, integrity or professionalism of Dix Performance North LTD.
  • Maintain the confidentiality of privileged information entrusted by Dix Performance North LTD.
  • Make reasonable efforts to minimize conflicts between events hosted by the Exhibitor and the Annual Trade Show's scheduled activities.
  • Wear appropriate apparel at all times. Business or business casual attire is suggested.
Damages

Dix Performance North LTD is not and shall not be liable for any damages, whether direct, indirect, general, special, consequential or otherwise, to the Attendee, its agents and employees or visitors to its trade show.

Code of conduct

Dix Performance North LTD is committed to the highest standard of respect and dignity at all of its events. All participants must conduct themselves in a safe and welcoming manner, free from harassment or discrimination.

Dix Performance North LTD does not tolerate harassment of any kind. Violations result in immediate revocation of registration and removal from the premises.
